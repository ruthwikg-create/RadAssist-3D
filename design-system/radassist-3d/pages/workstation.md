# RadAssist 3D — Workstation Page Override

- Use the global RadAssist 3D design system as the source of truth.
- Optimize for a dense radiology-style workstation, not a marketing dashboard.
- Prioritize the imaging viewport over decorative UI.
- Use Figtree for headings/UI labels and Noto Sans for body/supporting text.
- Maintain dark clinical contrast and visible 2px focus indicators.
- Use compact 40–44px controls with clear ARIA labels.
- Motion: 150–250ms micro-interactions; 300–450ms entry stagger; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Never use AI purple/pink gradients or emoji icons.
- Keep the disclaimer visible but visually subordinate to the imaging workspace.
